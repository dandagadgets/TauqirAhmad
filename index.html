Frequently asked questions

What is the consultation fee?

Rs. 1,500 per visit at Ayub Teaching Hospital. There's no extra charge for booking your appointment online.

What are the clinic timings?

Monday to Friday, 3:00 PM – 7:00 PM, at Ayub Teaching Hospital, Mandian, Abbottabad.

Can I have a video consultation instead of visiting in person?

Yes. Dr. Tauqir Ahmad offers both in-person consultations at the hospital and online video consultations, so you can choose whichever works best for you.

What conditions does Dr. Tauqir Ahmad treat?

As an Internal Medicine Specialist and Diabetologist, he treats high blood sugar, hypertension, fatigue, headaches, unexplained bleeding, muscle weakness, hormonal imbalance, infections, chronic pain, gastric problems, and other internal medicine conditions. See the Conditions section for the full list.

What are Dr. Tauqir Ahmad's qualifications?

MBBS from Ayub Medical College, FCPS Medicine from the College of Physicians & Surgeons Pakistan, and a Certification in Diabetes from Aga Khan University and the Royal College of Physicians, London. He is PMDC Verified and an Assistant Professor of Internal Medicine.
